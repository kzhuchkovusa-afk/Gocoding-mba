How It Works

Your Child's 9-Month Journey from Idea to Investor Pitch

A proven step-by-step system that transforms curious kids into confident young entrepreneurs

01

Months 1-2: Game On! Business Ideas

Business & Ideation

What they learn:

  • How to spot a problem that needs fixing (like a detective finding clues!)
  • Brainstorming cool ideas with friends (game-style team challenges)
  • Testing ideas before building them (no wasting time on bad ideas!)
  • Understanding what makes money vs. just having fun
Key Outcome: A validated business idea and a simple one-page plan: What problem? Who needs it? How do I make money?
Tools: Miro (brainstorming), Google Slides (presentations), Business Model Canvas
02

Months 3-6: Build Time!

Product Development (Step-by-Step, Everyone Succeeds!)

What they learn:

  • UI/UX Design: Making products people love to use
  • Prototyping: Turning ideas into real clickable designs
  • Coding Basics: Python or JavaScript fundamentals
  • Project Management: Breaking big dreams into doable steps
Key Outcome: A working Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of their app, game, or website
Tools: Figma (design), Replit (coding), Trello (project management)
03

Months 7-8: Time to Be Heard!

Marketing & Sales (Learn to Be Heard!)

What they learn:

  • Go-to-Market Strategy: How to find your first customers
  • Digital Marketing: Social media posts, emails, and content that works
  • Sales Funnels: Turning curious people into customers
  • Pitch Deck Creation: Telling your story in 10 powerful slides
  • The Key Skill: Articulating Yourself — Speaking clearly, confidently, and persuasively
Key Outcome: A complete investor pitch deck and a simple marketing plan
Tools: Canva (pitch deck design), Google Analytics (tracking), Mailchimp (email marketing)
04

Month 9: Demo Day

Competition & Graduation

What they learn:

  • Public Speaking: How to command a room (or a Zoom call)
  • Storytelling: Turning data into a compelling narrative
  • Handling Q&A: Answering tough questions with confidence
Key Outcome: A confident, polished pitch presented to a panel of real entrepreneurs for a chance to win $3,000 in seed funding.
Final: Graduation ceremony, certificates, and a portfolio piece your child can use for college applications or future opportunities.
